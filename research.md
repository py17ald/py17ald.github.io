# Research

This is my physics research page. Here you'll find my thesis and the articles I have produced.

## Background

For reference, I graduated with my BSc and MPhys in Theoretical Physics at the University of Leeds. My masters supervisor was [Dr Rob Purdy](https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/physics/staff/4128/dr-rob-purdy) and my research at this stage was in high energy physics. In particular, I investigated Lie groups beyond the standard model and GUT (Grand Unified theories) like SO(10) and E6. After obtaining a prestigious fellowship award - the Leeds Doctoral Scholarship - I subsequently pursued my PhD under the tutelage of [Professor Zlatko Papic](https://theory.leeds.ac.uk/zlatko-papic/) and [Professor Jiannis K. Pachos](https://theory.leeds.ac.uk/jiannis-pachos/).
